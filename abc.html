Why Eat a Balanced Diet?

Healthy eating is about variety, nourishment and enjoyment.

⚡

Everyday Energy

Carbohydrates provide energy for learning, playing and daily activities.

💪

Growth & Strength

Protein, calcium and other nutrients support growing bodies.

🧠

Brain Power

A variety of nourishing foods helps support concentration and brain health.

🌈

Food Variety

Different fruits, vegetables, grains and proteins offer different nutrients.

Build Your Balanced Plate 🍽️

Mix different food groups to make satisfying meals.

FRUITS & VEGGIES

Colorful Produce

Try carrots, spinach, tomatoes, bananas, apples and seasonal fruits.

GRAINS

Energy Foods

Enjoy rice, roti, oats, whole-wheat bread or other grains.

PROTEIN

Growing Strong

Include dal, beans, paneer, eggs, fish, chicken or nuts as suitable.

Remember: No single food makes a diet healthy. Variety and balance matter!

Simple Healthy Habits 🌱

Small everyday choices can make a difference.

💧

Drink Water

Keep water nearby and drink regularly throughout the day.

🥗

Eat Different Foods

Try different food groups and colors across your meals.

🍳

Don't Skip Meals

Eat regular, satisfying meals and snacks when you are hungry.

😴

Sleep Well

Good sleep supports learning, growth and overall well-being.

😊

Enjoy Your Food

All kinds of foods can fit into a balanced lifestyle.
